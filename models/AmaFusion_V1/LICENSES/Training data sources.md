# Training data sources

This model was fine-tuned on several openly licensed datasets.
No datasets are redistributed. Links are provided only for attribution.

---

**EasyPortrait**  
URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/gofixyourself/EasyPortrait
License: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) 
Used for: realistic faces and portraits
Changes: cropped, resized, quality filtered

**Manga109-s**  
URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/hal-utokyo/Manga109-s  
License: as stated on the dataset page
Used for: anime faces and full body
Changes: cropped, parsed, filtered

**Wikimedia Commons**  
URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/  
License: CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / Public Domain  
Used for: real faces and full body  
Changes: selection by license, cropping

**Synthetic anime NSFW**  
Self-generated (CC0)  
Used for: anime NSFW content  
Changes: generated and filtered
